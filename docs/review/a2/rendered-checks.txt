Note: Chrome's emulation of `forced-colors` alone draws a dark palette while still reporting a light colour scheme, which a
real high-contrast system does not do. The forced-colours screenshot was therefore captured with both `forced-colors: active`
and `prefers-color-scheme: dark` emulated.
